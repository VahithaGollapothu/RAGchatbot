=== ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR ADMISSION — GCET ===

1. B.E. / B.TECH PROGRAMS
---------------------------
Program: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
Program: Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
Program: Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE)
Program: Mechanical Engineering (MECH)
Program: Civil Engineering (CIVIL)
Program: Information Technology (IT)
Program: Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AIDS)

Minimum Eligibility:
  - Must have passed 10+2 or equivalent from a recognized board.
  - Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics (PCM) are mandatory.
  - Minimum aggregate marks: 60% (General/OC), 55% (BC/MBC), 50% (SC/ST/SCA).
  - No age limit for admission.
  - Candidates appearing in 12th board exams are also eligible to apply provisionally.

2. B.ARCH PROGRAM
------------------
  - 10+2 with Mathematics as a compulsory subject.
  - Minimum 50% marks in aggregate.
  - Must qualify NATA (National Aptitude Test in Architecture).
  - JEE Paper 2 score also accepted.

3. M.E. / M.TECH PROGRAMS
---------------------------
Program: Computer Science and Engineering
Program: VLSI Design
Program: Structural Engineering
Program: Power Systems Engineering

Minimum Eligibility:
  - B.E./B.Tech in relevant discipline from a recognized university.
  - Minimum CGPA of 6.5/10 or 60% aggregate marks.
  - TANCET score mandatory for Tamil Nadu candidates.
  - GATE score: Priority in merit list; additional scholarship benefits.

4. MBA PROGRAM
---------------
  - Any bachelor's degree (minimum 3 years) from a recognized university.
  - Minimum 50% aggregate marks.
  - TANCET MBA / CAT / MAT / XAT score required.
  - Work experience preferred but not mandatory.

5. MCA PROGRAM
---------------
  - BCA / B.Sc Computer Science / B.Sc IT or any graduate with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level.
  - Minimum 50% aggregate marks.
  - TANCET MCA score required.

6. LATERAL ENTRY (B.E./B.TECH — 2nd Year Direct Entry)
--------------------------------------------------------
  - Diploma holders in relevant branch from State Board of Technical Education.
  - Minimum 60% marks in Diploma.
  - B.Sc degree holders with Mathematics also eligible.
  - Admission through TNEA Lateral Entry counseling.
  - Seats: 20% of sanctioned intake (supernumerary seats).

7. NRI / PIO / FOREIGN NATIONALS
----------------------------------
  - Eligible for NRI quota seats (up to 5% of intake).
  - Equivalent qualification to 10+2 from their country.
  - Minimum 60% marks or equivalent GPA.
  - IELTS/TOEFL score required if medium of instruction was not English.
  - Apply directly to: nri.admissions@gcet.edu.in

8. PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES (PwD)
-------------------------------------
  - 5% horizontal reservation applicable.
  - Must have minimum 40% disability certified by a recognized government hospital.
  - Relaxation of 5% in minimum marks requirement.

9. SPORTS QUOTA
----------------
  - National/State level sports achievements considered.
  - Minimum 50% aggregate marks.
  - Original certificates from Sports Authority must be submitted.
