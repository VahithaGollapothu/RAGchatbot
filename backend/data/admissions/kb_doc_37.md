### Q1: What is the eligibility for B.Tech admission?

**Answer:** Students must complete 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics with a minimum of 60% aggregate marks.

### Q2: Can diploma students apply for lateral entry?

**Answer:** Yes, diploma holders with at least 50% marks can apply directly to the second year through lateral entry admissions.

### Q3: What documents are required during admission?

**Answer:** Students must submit SSC memo, Intermediate marks memo, transfer certificate, income certificate, Aadhaar copy, and passport-size photographs.
