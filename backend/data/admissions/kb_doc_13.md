# Placements Knowledge Base Document 13

## Department
Placements

## Additional Topic
Fee Payments

### Q1: How can students register for placement drives?

**Answer:** Students must register through the Training and Placement Cell portal before the registration deadline.

### Q2: Who can attend campus placements?

**Answer:** Students with no active backlogs and meeting company eligibility criteria can attend placement drives.

### Q3: Are internships mandatory?

**Answer:** Yes, all final-year students must complete at least one internship before graduation.

## Student Support Notes

Students are encouraged to regularly check the college portal for announcements, deadlines, and policy updates. For urgent support, contact the respective department office.
