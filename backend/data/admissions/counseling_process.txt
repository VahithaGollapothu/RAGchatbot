=== COUNSELING PROCESS — GCET ADMISSIONS ===

1. TNEA COUNSELING OVERVIEW
-----------------------------
TNEA Counseling is conducted entirely online by Anna University. Students don't need to visit Anna University for counseling — they participate from home.

2. STEP-BY-STEP COUNSELING PROCESS
-------------------------------------
STEP 1: Registration & Payment
  - Visit tnea.ac.in and register with your 12th mark details.
  - Pay registration fee online: Rs. 500 (General), Rs. 250 (SC/ST)
  - Upload required documents as PDF.

STEP 2: Rank Publication
  - TNEA publishes normalized rank based on HSC marks.
  - Check rank on tnea.ac.in using your application number.

STEP 3: Choice Filling (Online)
  - Log in to TNEA portal during choice filling window.
  - Add colleges and branches in order of preference (up to 50 choices).
  - GCET College Code: TN-1075
  - Save and lock choices before deadline.

STEP 4: Seat Allotment
  - Seats allotted based on rank, category, and preference order.
  - Allotment letter downloadable from portal.

STEP 5: Reporting to College
  - Download and print allotment order.
  - Report to GCET Admissions Office within deadline (3 working days).
  - Bring all original documents.
  - Pay first-year fees at the college fee counter.

STEP 6: Enrollment Completion
  - Receive Student ID Number.
  - ID card issued within 3 working days.
  - Classes begin as per schedule.

3. CATEGORIES IN TNEA COUNSELING
----------------------------------
  - OC (Open Category / General): 30.5% seats
  - BC: 26.5% seats
  - BCM: 3.5% seats
  - MBC/DNC: 20% seats
  - SC: 15% seats
  - SCA: 3% seats
  - ST: 1% seats
  - Government School Preference: 7.5% of seats reserved for government school students

4. SPECIAL COUNSELING ROUNDS
------------------------------
Round 1: Main counseling (July)
Round 2: Second counseling for unfilled seats (July)
Round 3 (Spot Admission): For any remaining seats (August)
  - Physical attendance at designated venue required for Spot Admission.
  - Bring all original documents and DD for fee payment.

5. WHAT HAPPENS IF YOU MISS THE DEADLINE?
------------------------------------------
  - Allotted seat is automatically cancelled.
  - No re-allotment in the same round.
  - Can participate in next round if available.
  - Management quota seats may be available after all rounds.

6. MANAGEMENT QUOTA COUNSELING (College-Level)
------------------------------------------------
  - 15% of seats filled through management quota.
  - Contact GCET admissions office directly.
  - Merit considered: HSC marks + JEE score (if available).
  - No direct donation/capitation fee accepted (illegal).
