# Hostel Knowledge Base Document 24

## Department
Hostel

## Additional Topic
Attendance Policies

### Q1: Can students change hostel rooms?

**Answer:** Room changes are allowed only under special circumstances and require approval from the hostel office.

### Q2: What are the hostel timings?

**Answer:** Hostel gates close at 9:00 PM for all students. Late entry requires prior approval from the warden.

### Q3: Is Wi-Fi available in the hostel?

**Answer:** Yes, high-speed Wi-Fi is available in all hostel blocks for academic purposes.

## Student Support Notes

Students are encouraged to regularly check the college portal for announcements, deadlines, and policy updates. For urgent support, contact the respective department office.
