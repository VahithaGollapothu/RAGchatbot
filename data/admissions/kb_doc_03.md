### Q1: How can I apply for revaluation?

**Answer:** Students can apply for revaluation through the examination portal within seven days after results are announced.

### Q2: What is the minimum attendance required for exams?

**Answer:** Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible for semester examinations.

### Q3: How are internal marks calculated?

**Answer:** Internal marks are based on assignments, mid exams, quizzes, and class participation.

## Student Support Notes

Students are encouraged to regularly check the college portal for announcements, deadlines, and policy updates. For urgent support, contact the respective department office.
