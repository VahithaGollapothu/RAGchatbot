=== ADMISSION PROCESS AT GREENFIELD COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING AND TECHNOLOGY ===

1. OVERVIEW
-----------
Greenfield College of Engineering and Technology (GCET) follows a transparent and merit-based admission process for all its undergraduate (B.E./B.Tech) and postgraduate (M.E./M.Tech/MBA/MCA) programs. The process is governed by the rules and regulations set by the State Government, Anna University, and the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).

2. UNDERGRADUATE ADMISSION (B.E./B.Tech)
-----------------------------------------
Step 1: Appear for qualifying examinations
  - Candidates must complete 12th standard (HSC) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as core subjects.
  - Minimum aggregate: 60% for General category, 55% for BC/OBC, 50% for SC/ST categories.
  
Step 2: Appear for entrance examinations
  - Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions (TNEA) conducted by Anna University.
  - Admission is based on 12th marks normalized score for TNEA counseling.
  - Candidates may also apply under JEE Main for NRI/Management quota seats.
  
Step 3: Register on the official TNEA portal
  - Visit: tnea.ac.in
  - Fill online application, upload documents, and pay registration fee of Rs. 500.
  
Step 4: Attend counseling
  - First, Second, and Third round counseling sessions are held.
  - Physical reporting at designated counseling centers is mandatory.
  - Rank-based allocation of seats.
  
Step 5: Report to the college
  - Produce original certificates for verification.
  - Pay first-year tuition fee within 3 days of allotment.
  - Complete enrollment formalities.

3. POSTGRADUATE ADMISSION (M.E./M.Tech)
-----------------------------------------
  - TANCET (Tamil Nadu Common Entrance Test) score required.
  - GATE score holders get priority in merit list.
  - Direct admission possible through management quota for GATE qualified candidates.

4. MBA/MCA ADMISSION
---------------------
  - TANCET MBA/MCA score required.
  - Group discussion and personal interview conducted at the college.
  - Final merit list prepared based on TANCET score (70%) + GD/PI (30%).
