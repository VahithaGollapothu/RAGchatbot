=== ENTRANCE EXAM INFORMATION — GCET ADMISSIONS ===

1. TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions)
--------------------------------------------
Conducting Body: Anna University, Chennai
Type: Rank-based (No written exam; based on HSC marks)

Calculation:
  - Normalized HSC aggregate score in PCM
  - Formula: (Physics + Chemistry + Mathematics) / 3 × weight factor
  - Rank assigned based on normalized score

Process:
  - Register on tnea.ac.in
  - Submit HSC marks
  - Rank allocated
  - Participate in online counseling

Cutoff Trends (Approximate for GCET 2024):
  - CSE: 155+ marks (out of 200)
  - ECE: 145+ marks
  - EEE: 140+ marks
  - MECH: 130+ marks
  - CIVIL: 125+ marks
  - IT: 150+ marks
  - AIDS: 152+ marks

2. JEE MAIN (for NRI/Management Quota)
----------------------------------------
Conducting Body: National Testing Agency (NTA)
Frequency: Twice a year (January and April sessions)
Eligibility: 12th with PCM
Exam Mode: Computer-based
Duration: 3 hours
Sections: Mathematics (30Q), Physics (30Q), Chemistry (30Q)
Max Score: 300

JEE Score Use at GCET:
  - NRI Quota seats
  - Management Quota (if TNEA rank insufficient)
  - Scholarship basis for high scorers

3. TANCET (Tamil Nadu Common Entrance Test)
--------------------------------------------
Conducting Body: Anna University, Chennai
Programs: M.E./M.Tech, MBA, MCA

TANCET M.E./M.Tech:
  - Duration: 2 hours
  - Sections: Engineering Mathematics, Basic Engineering & Sciences, Subject-specific
  - Total: 100 marks

TANCET MBA:
  - Duration: 2 hours
  - Sections: Data Sufficiency, Reading Comprehension, Verbal Ability, Quantitative Ability, Analytical Reasoning
  - Total: 100 marks

TANCET MCA:
  - Duration: 2 hours
  - Sections: Mathematics, Computer Awareness, Analytical Reasoning, Verbal Ability
  - Total: 100 marks

4. GATE (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering)
-------------------------------------------------
Conducting Body: IITs/IISc (rotational)
Validity: 3 years
Use at GCET: Priority in M.E./M.Tech merit list + scholarship benefits

GATE Benefits at GCET:
  - GATE scorers get first priority in PG admissions
  - GATE Score > 500: 25% tuition fee waiver for M.E./M.Tech
  - GATE Score > 600: Teaching Assistantship of Rs. 5000/month

5. NATA (National Aptitude Test in Architecture)
-------------------------------------------------
Required For: B.Arch program
Conducting Body: Council of Architecture (COA)
Minimum Score: 80 out of 200 for eligibility

6. PREPARATION TIPS
---------------------
  - For TNEA: Focus on PCM subjects in 12th standard; higher marks = better rank
  - For TANCET: Practice previous year papers (available at tancet.annauniv.edu)
  - For GATE: Refer standard textbooks + NPTEL online courses
  - For JEE: Practice mock tests on NTA Abhyas app
