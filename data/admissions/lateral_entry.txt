=== LATERAL ENTRY ADMISSION — GCET ===

1. WHAT IS LATERAL ENTRY?
--------------------------
Lateral Entry is a scheme that allows diploma holders and B.Sc graduates to directly join the 2nd year (3rd semester) of B.E./B.Tech programs, bypassing the 1st year. This fast-tracks eligible candidates into engineering programs.

2. ELIGIBILITY FOR LATERAL ENTRY
----------------------------------
a) Diploma Holders:
   - Must have passed 3-year Diploma in Engineering/Technology from a recognized State Board of Technical Education (DOTE).
   - Minimum marks: 60% aggregate (General), 55% (BC/MBC), 50% (SC/ST).
   - Branch of diploma should be relevant to the B.E./B.Tech program applied for.

b) B.Sc Degree Holders:
   - B.Sc graduates with Mathematics as a subject are eligible.
   - Minimum 60% marks in B.Sc.
   - Can join B.E./B.Tech (CSE, IT, ECE) programs.

c) Diploma in Pharmacy:
   - Eligible for lateral entry into B.Pharm 2nd year (if applicable).

3. SEAT MATRIX
---------------
Total Lateral Entry Seats: 20% of approved intake (supernumerary seats)
Example:
  - CSE (intake 120): Lateral Entry = 24 seats
  - ECE (intake 60): Lateral Entry = 12 seats
  - MECH (intake 60): Lateral Entry = 12 seats

4. ADMISSION PROCESS
---------------------
Step 1: Register on TNEA Lateral Entry portal (tnea.ac.in)
Step 2: Upload diploma/B.Sc certificates and mark sheets
Step 3: Pay registration fee: Rs. 300
Step 4: Attend counseling as per rank
Step 5: Report to college with original documents

5. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
----------------------
  - 10th mark sheet and certificate
  - Diploma/B.Sc all semester mark sheets
  - Diploma/Degree certificate or Provisional certificate
  - Transfer Certificate (TC) from previous institution
  - Community Certificate (for reservation candidates)
  - Conduct Certificate
  - Passport-size photos (6 copies)
  - Aadhaar card copy

6. CURRICULUM ADJUSTMENT
--------------------------
  - Lateral entry students join directly in Semester 3.
  - Additional bridge courses are offered in Semester 3 for subjects covered in Semester 1 & 2:
    * Engineering Mathematics I & II (as bridge course)
    * Engineering Physics / Chemistry concepts
    * Basic Programming (for non-CS diploma students entering CS)

7. IMPORTANT NOTES
-------------------
  - Lateral entry candidates are NOT eligible for hostel in the first year of their lateral entry (limited availability).
  - Scholarship eligibility: Government scholarships apply; merit scholarships are evaluated from Semester 3 onwards.
  - Lateral entry students must clear all bridge courses to be eligible for semester exams.

  - Reporting Deadline: August 20, 2025
