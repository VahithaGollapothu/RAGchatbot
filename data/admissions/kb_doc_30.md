# Scholarships Knowledge Base Document 30

## Department
Scholarships

## Additional Topic
Attendance Policies

### Q1: How can I apply for a merit scholarship?

**Answer:** Students can apply through the student portal after semester results are announced. Scholarships are granted based on CGPA and attendance.

### Q2: Who is eligible for fee reimbursement?

**Answer:** Students belonging to eligible income categories as per state government norms can apply for fee reimbursement.

### Q3: When are scholarship amounts credited?

**Answer:** Scholarship amounts are usually credited within 45 to 60 days after verification approval.

## Student Support Notes

Students are encouraged to regularly check the college portal for announcements, deadlines, and policy updates. For urgent support, contact the respective department office.
