=== REQUIRED DOCUMENTS FOR ADMISSION — GCET ===

Please ensure ALL original documents are available at the time of reporting. Photocopies must be self-attested.

1. DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR B.E./B.TECH ADMISSION
-------------------------------------------------
Original Documents (to be submitted and returned after verification):
  [1] SSLC / 10th Standard Mark Sheet (Original + 3 photocopies)
  [2] HSC / 12th Standard Mark Sheet (Original + 3 photocopies)
  [3] HSC / 12th Standard Certificate (Pass Certificate)
  [4] Transfer Certificate (TC) from last attended institution (Original only)
  [5] Conduct Certificate from last institution
  [6] Community/Caste Certificate (Original + 2 photocopies) — for reservation
  [7] Income Certificate (if applying for fee concession/scholarship)
  [8] Aadhaar Card (Original + 2 photocopies)
  [9] Passport-size photographs — 10 copies (white background, recent)
  [10] TNEA Allotment Order printout
  [11] Migration Certificate (if from outside Tamil Nadu board)
  [12] Medical Fitness Certificate from a registered medical practitioner

For Special Categories:
  [13] Nativity Certificate (for TN government quota)
  [14] Sports Achievement Certificate (for Sports Quota)
  [15] PwD Certificate from Government Hospital (for PwD Quota)
  [16] Ex-Serviceman Certificate (if applicable)
  [17] NRI/OCI/PIO Card copy (for NRI Quota)

2. DOCUMENTS FOR M.E./M.TECH ADMISSION
----------------------------------------
  [1] B.E./B.Tech all semester mark sheets (Original + 3 copies)
  [2] B.E./B.Tech Degree Certificate / Provisional Certificate
  [3] TANCET/GATE Score Card
  [4] 10th and 12th Mark Sheets
  [5] Transfer Certificate, Conduct Certificate
  [6] Community Certificate
  [7] Aadhaar Card
  [8] 6 passport-size photos
  [9] Experience Certificate (if applicable)

3. DOCUMENTS FOR MBA/MCA ADMISSION
------------------------------------
  [1] All UG semester mark sheets (Original + 3 copies)
  [2] UG Degree Certificate / Provisional Certificate
  [3] TANCET/CAT/MAT Score Card
  [4] GD/PI Call Letter
  [5] 10th, 12th Mark Sheets
  [6] Transfer Certificate
  [7] Community Certificate
  [8] Aadhaar Card
  [9] Work Experience Letter (if any)
  [10] 6 passport-size photos

4. DOCUMENT VERIFICATION PROCESS
----------------------------------
Step 1: Submit documents at the Admissions Counter (Block A, Ground Floor).
Step 2: Documents verified by Admission Officer — takes 30–45 minutes.
Step 3: Receive verification receipt.
Step 4: Proceed to Fee Payment Counter.
Step 5: Complete enrollment and receive Student ID card (within 3 working days).

5. IMPORTANT NOTES
-------------------
  - Originals are returned after verification. Do NOT send originals by post.
  - Incomplete documents will result in cancellation of admission.
  - Submitting false/forged documents will result in immediate expulsion and legal action.
  - Document verification timing: Monday–Friday 9 AM–4 PM, Saturday 9 AM–1 PM.
